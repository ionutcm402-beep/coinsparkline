# Phase 8 — Secondary Discovery Product Gate

## Privacy routes

- `/privacy` is the legal Privacy Policy. It is not a market product.
- `/privacy-coins` is Privacy Intelligence. Its primary question is: **How do major privacy-focused assets differ in privacy model, technology and current market behaviour?**
- Privacy Intelligence must not become a generic coin screener. Its differentiated value is technology-model comparison plus CoinSparkLine market context.

## NFT Radar

- `/nft` is NFT market discovery. Its primary question is: **Which NFT collections are showing meaningful marketplace activity, and what should I verify before opening the marketplace?**
- It must remain focused on collection liquidity/activity, floor, sales and marketplace verification rather than reproducing fungible-asset SparkScore screening.

## Product rule

A specialist discovery page survives only when it answers a distinct user question that the core Screener, Radar, Compare or Coin Detail products do not already answer.

## Visual rule

Secondary discovery products use the permanent CoinSparkLine language: neutral canvas, restrained white surfaces, semantic state colour, minimal shadows and no decorative gradient hierarchy.
